WHAT WE BUILD

From the PCB to the host application.

Our products address a practical integration problem: a computer needs to communicate with equipment that uses different electrical interfaces. We provide CAN FD and serial connections as interface ICs, internal modules, and enclosed USB hardware.

The product work covers interface firmware, PCB hardware, device drivers, control software, and communication diagnostics. Smart-battery interfaces extend the same approach to battery information and host-system visibility.
