Battery compatibility

This family is intended for supported SBS-compatible smart batteries. Battery pinout, voltage levels, pull-ups, supported commands, and connector wiring must be confirmed before connection.

Planned information

Voltage, current, temperature, state of charge, remaining capacity, full-charge capacity, cycle count, and battery status.

Not a charger

This product reads and reports battery information. Charging or battery-protection functions are not implied.

Development stage

SMBus communication, supported commands, USB behavior, and host software are still being validated.
