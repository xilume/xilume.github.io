INTERNAL CAN FD

Two CAN networks. Inside the PC.

DualCANFD occupies a compatible Mini PCIe slot, keeping the interface board and host connection inside the chassis. The example shows the module beside the computer’s storage device.

Host connection
Mini PCIe slot with USB 2.0 signals
Added interfaces
2 independent CAN / CAN FD channels with signal isolation
Software

INTERNAL MULTIBUS

A communication board. Built into the machine.

XE826 mounts on standoffs inside the enclosure and connects to an internal USB header. In this installation, ribbon cables bring the CAN connections to two panel DB9 connectors, while the serial terminals remain accessible through the chassis.

Host connection
Internal USB header
Added interfaces
2 × CAN FD · 4 × RS-485 · 2 × RS-232
Installation
Board inside, field connections at the panel
