MULTI-INTERFACE HUB

Eight communication channels. One USB connection.

Xilume 8Hub is being developed to bring two CAN FD, four RS-485, and two RS-232 channels into one interface for mixed-bus development, commissioning, and equipment integration.

WHY 8HUB

A cleaner way to work with mixed serial and CAN systems.

Several separate adapters quickly create cable clutter, inconsistent labels, multiple enclosures, and a more difficult service workflow.
01

Reduce adapter clutter

Bring the eight planned communication channels into one organized desktop or equipment interface.

02

Keep every bus visible

The planned channel map keeps CAN FD, RS-485, and RS-232 groups separate and clearly identified for faster connection and troubleshooting.

03

Work across mixed equipment

Connect controllers, sensors, instruments, and service ports from the same host system.
