02SIGNAL ARCHITECTURE

One host link.
Two independent field buses.

The card uses USB 2.0 signals routed through the Mini PCIe connector. The two CAN paths remain separate from the controller to the external networks.

Important host requirement

The connector shape alone does not guarantee compatibility. The host must route USB 2.0 signals to its Mini PCIe slot; a PCIe-lane-only slot cannot communicate with this module.
