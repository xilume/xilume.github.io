BEFORE YOU GET IN TOUCH

What to include in your enquiry

Choosing or purchasing a product

  • Product model, or interface types and channel counts
  • USB or Mini PCIe host connection, and internal or external installation
  • Operating system, application, and available installation space
  • Required quantity, destination, and project schedule

An integration or communication issue

  • Model, host OS, CPU architecture, and Linux kernel where applicable
  • Driver and application versions, plus device-recognition screenshots
  • Wiring photos, bit rates, termination, and connected equipment
  • Expected result, observed behavior, reproduction steps, and available logs
