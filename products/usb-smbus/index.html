BATTERY INFORMATION

Reported battery data

Available fields follow the commands supported by the connected battery pack.
01State of chargeRemaining percentage and operating state
02Voltage + currentPack voltage and charge/discharge direction
03RuntimeRemaining-time information when available
04CapacityRemaining and full-charge capacity
05TemperaturePack-reported thermal information
06Status + cyclesCycle count, status, and health indicators
Status interface only.

Battery charging, protection, and power-path functions are separate from the battery display function.
